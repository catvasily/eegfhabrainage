* Note on PCA transformation:
    . The strongest PC component essentially summates all channels with almost
      equal weights. Its time course values may easily exceed 10 Hz. because 
      those are in fact sums of deviations from means for each channel

    . There is no sense in adding transformed means to the PCs, because for example
      the mean corresponding to the strongest component will be much larger than
      a typical channel mean. Example with all equal channels:
        pc0 = {1/nchan^1/2}
        pc0_mean = (ch_mean/nchan^1/2)*n_chan = nchan^1/2 * ch_mean
      Thus pc0_mean does not reflect a typical channel mean but is nchan^1/2 larger.

* NOTE: light travels from Sun to Earth ~8 min. However solar wind speed is
  ~400 km/s. Thus solar wind travels from Sun to Earth ~4.3 days, or ~104 hours

* Downloaded full hourly data for all years from omni site:
        omni2_all_years.dat, 171 Mb
  then manually deleted all rows for years before 2005 and after 2019 using
  office calc. The resulting file
  is
        omni2_2005-2019.dat, 31 Mb

  To achieve this in libre office, use
    - Ctrl-H to open search box; then select the years column, specify the
      year you want, and check "Selected area only" or like that.
      This way desired year rows can be found

    - Use Data/Define range, then Data/Select range, to specify area one
      wants to delete by area coords

    - Use Sheet/Delete Rows to delete the area

* In omni_ files, the hour column is #3 (1-based); _01_av.dat and _27_av.dat
  files have 0 in that column. However value 0 cannot be interpreted as
  NaN because this is a valid hour number in hour-averaged files.

* After implementing time shift support, decided to drop using omni
  .._01_av.dat files (that is, day averaged files), because they have
  00:00 in the time data. So:
